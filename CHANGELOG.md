## v0.1.0 (2026-11-25)

First release.

- Daily forecast with high and low temperatures, hourly forecast with temperature colored bubbles
- Two weather icon packs
- Visual editor
- Forecast loaded through the `weather/subscribe_forecast` subscription: live updates
- Tap, hold and double tap actions, executed by Home Assistant (all action types supported)
- Card preview in the card picker, the first weather entity is preselected
